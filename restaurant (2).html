Our Story

Born from Passion

This is the world's best restaurant of all time.

JAMIA was born in 2010 from a single obsession: to make every plate a moment worth remembering. Chef Isabelle Fontaine opened these doors with nothing more than a farm-to-table philosophy and relentless pursuit of perfection.

Today, our kitchen team of 22 travels the globe for the finest ingredients — truffle from Périgord, saffron from Kashmir, wagyu from Kagoshima — weaving them into dishes that honor tradition while celebrating bold imagination.
